# 📊 Sales Performance & Customer Analysis — Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to identify key business trends, top-performing products, customer behavior, and overall sales performance.

The project demonstrates practical Excel data-analysis skills, including **data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive dashboard creation**.

The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to provide a quick overview of business performance and help identify areas for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

* How is overall sales performance trending?
* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which products sell the highest quantities?
* Which categories perform best?
* Which customers contribute the most revenue?
* How does performance vary over time?
* What are the key areas driving overall business performance?

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

Analyzed overall revenue and sales performance using PivotTables and summary metrics.

Key metrics include:

* Total Revenue
* Total Quantity Sold
* Number of Orders
* Average Order Value

### 2. Product Analysis

Identified products contributing the most to overall revenue and sales volume.

Analysis included:

* Top 10 Products by Revenue
* Top Products by Quantity Sold
* Product-level revenue contribution
* Product performance comparison

### 3. Category Analysis

Compared sales performance across product categories to identify the strongest and weakest-performing areas.

### 4. Customer Analysis

Analyzed customer-level sales to identify high-value customers and understand revenue concentration.

### 5. Time-Based Analysis

Examined sales trends over time to identify changes in revenue and sales volume.

## 📊 Dashboard

The final Excel dashboard brings the analysis together into an interactive reporting interface.

The dashboard includes:

* KPI Cards
* Revenue Analysis
* Product Performance
* Category Performance
* Customer Analysis
* Time-Based Trends
* Interactive Filters / Slicers

The dashboard is designed to allow users to quickly identify important business trends without manually analyzing the underlying dataset.
